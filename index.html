Tech, AI & side projects

Engines, neuroevolution, agents, tools, and homebrew.

AI⇄

Santa Barbara Games · Coding agents · in-game sub-agents

Agentic AI workflows

A human asks a coding agent for a change. The coding agent then commands sub-agents inside the running game to exercise it, verify the results, and do their own testing. I also built workflows that let non-coders ship changes.

SMU Guildhall · C++ · OpenGL / DirectX · Lua

JanssEngine

My own engine and renderer, built from the ground up: dual OpenGL/DirectX renderer, Lua integration, my own JanScript language, LOD terrain, BSP with PVS, and hot-reloadable DLL plugins.

Details →

AI project · NEAT

NEAT auto-balancer

Neuroevolution that balances a game. It takes every unit's health and attack damage as input and outputs attack cooldowns that hit a desired lock/unlock chart. Agents earn fitness per balanced unit and category (max 32), and the run stops when one balances the whole game.

AI competition · Agent AI

AI Battle — 2nd place

A battle for food and survival between programmed AIs. My cockroach-like agents build and share a light map and try to move toward darkness. Walls, enemies, and dirt emit light, and found food emits darkness toward the nearest unit. Took 2nd place overall.

B!

2-person AI class project · Java

Braaains!

A top-down 2D zombie shooter whose zombies use neuroevolution to adapt to how you play in real time. Sensors for the player and bullets go in, and rotation, velocity, dodges, and lunges come out.

Image recognition · Computer vision

Robot Vision

An edge and line detection pipeline: Sobel for gradient magnitude and direction, then Canny for peaks and valleys, then Burns for the N longest lines within an angle threshold.

Guildhall · jams · Box2D · Lua · XNA

Editors & pipelines

A convex-polygon collision editor for Box2D and an animation-cell editor, both with live previews and Lua export. Also a data-driven tilemap pipeline that had designers building Johny Apple Siege maps within 2 hours of a 36-hour jam.

Community project · Wii / Wii U homebrew

Project M 3.0

The community project to make Brawl feel more like Melee. I worked on the homebrew front-end launcher and updater.

05

About

I'm a game programmer who loves the parts players feel: tight controls, juicy feedback, rock-solid multiplayer, and frame times that never hitch. I've led Skyrim VR from conception to launch, led Starfield's StarMap, and today I own Voidwood's deterministic rollback simulation at Santa Barbara Games.

Lately I've been building agentic AI workflows where coding agents drive in-game sub-agents to test and verify their own work. To me, reading code and understanding exactly what it does at a low level now matters more than typing it.

Off the clock I'm a competitive gamer (8th of ~80,000 in GameStop's Super Smash Bros. Brawl national grand finals; Evolve Top 100 Goliath), a game jam regular, and happiest on a river in a canoe.
